# Level 4 source review

All 33 [cellular catalogue cards](../level-4.json) have explicit scope contracts. Each substantive assertion and every proposed parent occurrence has a supported, qualified, excluded or unresolved disposition. This is an accounting of the admitted evidence, not proof that every original assertion is correct or that the experiments have been independently reproduced.

Levels group the display. They do not establish an evolutionary sequence, an ontology of universal necessities or a construction recipe for living cells. A bounded response in a modern supplied preparation does not establish the origin of that preparation.

The cellular module contains **255 records, 228 claims, 397 relations, 70 preparation-specific study contexts and 15 comparisons**. Its 47 source records are supplemented by already admitted sources where a card refers to the same evidence. Multiple preparations, cards or measurements from one publication do not constitute independent replication.

The 172 assertion dispositions are 38 supported, 75 qualified, 33 excluded, 26 unresolved. The 155 parent dispositions are 0 supported, 105 qualified, 20 excluded, 30 unresolved. These counts describe review scope, not confidence scores.

The [scope contracts](../../models/causal-emergence/canonical/level-four.mjs) retain assertion and parent decisions with their reasons and claim identities. The [cellular evidence ledger](cellular-review.json) records preparations, observables, uncertainty, reading limits and publication dependence; the canonical claims retain exact citation locators. The matrix below points to selected primary evidence; the complete source lists remain with the claims. A qualified parent is a bounded scientific relationship to assess; its scope contract does not by itself add a physical dependency edge.

## Admission rules

- **Supported:** a source supports the named claim within its stated system, intervention, observable and reading extent.
- **Qualified:** the broad statement requires a narrower interpretation or has substantial preparation, measurement or mechanism limits.
- **Excluded:** the proposed assertion or dependency is not admitted as stated. Exclusion is not a claim that no relevant phenomenon exists anywhere.
- **Unresolved:** a specific hypothesis or coverage obligation remains open. Unknown mechanisms and unread controls remain unknown.

Definitions organize terms and carry no invented experimental context. Preparation/method claims remain separate from published observations and conditional inferences. Functional-support relations require matched source evidence at both endpoints in the actual preparation; they do not establish universal necessity. Scope membership and interpretation links do not become physical interactions.

Uncalibrated parent weights, carrier identifiers and minima, scalar necessity labels, catalogue phase/role labels and SOMA classifications are not admitted as biological measurements. Broad bibliographies identify topics; the actual primary passages support the scoped claims. No numerical replay, raw-data reanalysis, exhaustive correction audit or independent expert replication is asserted.

## Scientific interpretation

The graph now supports comparisons of conditional organization: genotype-dependent division, selective compartment exchange, cargo-dependent uptake, finite feedback responses and intervention-dependent cellular states. These cases make the interaction-first strategy testable because the participants, perturbations, readouts and limits can be examined separately. General principles remain candidate patterns whose transfer must be checked in independent preparations and publications.

- Cellular minimality remains conditional on genotype, medium and the measured function. Population propagation, complete scission, membrane enclosure and near-normal morphology are different endpoints; a requirement in one reduced genotype need not hold in another.
- Compartment and pathway organization is dynamic. Marker lifetime, optical overlap, cargo uptake, intracellular movement and secretion into collected medium require distinct observations. A marker can persist while function changes.
- ATP concentration, ATP flux, proton-motive force, voltage, pH and motor work are distinct quantities. Cross-publication observations cannot be combined into one measured whole-cell energy balance.
- Reporter distributions, finite state persistence, model bistability and irreversible differentiation are different claims. A source conflict is retained when text, figure captions or construct labels disagree.
- DNA repair products do not establish complete genome stability; pathway fractions depend on the assay. Chromatophore sequence inventory, evolutionary reconstruction, protein processing and the mechanism of membrane crossing remain separate evidence classes.
- Feedback, state transitions, stress responses, immune signaling and sensory adaptation are preparation-specific. A pathway drawing does not measure amplification, an observed state does not uniquely identify its mechanism, and a sensor response does not by itself demonstrate successful long-term adaptation.
- Cellular degradation, autophagy markers, lysosomal flux, cargo elimination, nutrient reuse, growth arrest and secretory phenotypes require separate endpoints. Their association does not establish a single universal survival program or a single senescence mechanism.
- Phototransduction evidence separates biochemical activation, channel assays, intact rod current and GCAP feedback. The Fung correction preserves the distinct reaction requirements; the GCAP knockout removes detectable calcium dependence of cyclase activity rather than the enzyme or every basal activity. Residual adaptation depends on a model comparison and has no uniquely established molecular mechanism here.

## Remaining scientific questions

Completion of a source disposition leaves a question explicit rather than silently treating it as established. The current open boundaries include:

- Simultaneous closure of membrane maintenance, metabolic supply, information processing and division in the same specified minimal-cell preparation.
- Mechanistic identification where an assay measures a proxy: reporter fluorescence, optical overlap, membrane dyes, pathway FRET, repair-probe loss and compartment-marker trajectories.
- Whether candidate feedback, barrier/resource and joint-organization patterns transfer to independent publications and contrasting preparations. Shared papers and shared acquisitions cannot supply an independent transfer test.
- Distinguishing finite persistence from heritable state, marker abundance from functional capacity, degradation from biosynthetic reuse, and expression signatures from a unique cell-wide regulatory mechanism.
- Matched whole-system measurements before assigning end-to-end gain, noise suppression, full energy balance, universal necessity or a generative emergence rule.
