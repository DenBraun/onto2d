For each card:

1. Read the complete source, its proposed parents and relevant existing canonical
   evidence. Separate substantive claims from SOMA classifications and numerical
   defaults; do not inherit weights, necessity or carrier minima as physical laws.
2. Choose a finite system and observable. Read actual primary-source passages,
   check publication identity and corrections, and record the reading extent.
   Reuse existing evidence with its original scope and shared-data dependencies.
3. Represent definitions, preparations, response models, observations and
   conditional inferences separately. State uncertainty, alternatives and
   unresolved limitations with the affected claims.
4. Update the canonical graph, representation roles and relevant review data.
   Remove a pending entry only after all original assertions have dispositions.
   Protect material interpretation boundaries with meaningful regression checks.
5. Rebuild and verify the current release, registry and browser projection within
   transport budgets. Preserve original inputs and registered frozen releases.
   Run focused checks, ordinary tests and the build before reporting readiness.
