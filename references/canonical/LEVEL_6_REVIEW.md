# Level 6 source review

All 25 [neural-system catalogue cards](../level-6.json) have explicit scientific
scope decisions. The [canonical review](neural-systems-review.json) retains all
237 assertion decisions and 99 ordered parent occurrences, their evidence
references, excluded defaults and open questions. These are current scientific
contracts, not a claim that cognition or a universal emergence sequence has been
derived. Source-card completion leaves unknown mechanisms unknown.

The assertion census is 31 supported, 114 qualified, 44 excluded and 48 unresolved.
The parent census is 39 qualified, one excluded and 59 unresolved. No original
parent is admitted as a universally necessary, weighted physical dependency.
These counts measure source coverage, not confidence, causal strength or
independent replication.

The reconstruction adds 69 entities, 69 claims and 696 relations: 688 descriptive
and eight preparation-bound functional links. The descriptive links organize
definitions, source observations, nulls and hypotheses; they do not turn them
into physical causes. Existing neural, visual, cellular and organismal studies
retain their identities. Twelve additional preparation/readout contexts come
from four primary placebo-related papers, with five scoped comparisons. The
eight additional publication records comprise those four papers, a secondary
framework review, two books with bibliographic-only scope and a conceptual
medical-model article read at abstract level. A publication record does not
imply a primary experiment or a complete reading.

## Scientific distinctions

An anatomical connection, effective response, correlated population activity
and successful behavior answer different questions. Plasticity is not every
form of learning; an oscillation is not criticality; a decoder does not identify
a unique circuit or physical minimum. Conscious experience, access, task report
and visual-feature readouts remain distinct. Subjective relief, pain tolerance,
motor timing, hormone concentration, immune-cell assays and spirometry are
different measurement functions.

Card 6.16 is assessed as neurovascular coupling, as named in its title and
vascular references. Its immune description and proposed dependencies cannot
supply vascular mechanisms. Every inherited parent receives an individual
decision. Vessel diameter, red-cell flow, tissue fluorescence and inferred
neuronal demand remain distinct; vascular activity is not a direct neural
readout. Card 6.21's parent 5.9 denotes immune defense, not generic nervous
integration; an IL-2 response does not make immune defense a necessary cause of
every pain, hormonal or symptom response.

Denman 2015's selected preparation Methods identify acute, anesthetized adult
GN220 Ntsr1-Cre mouse recordings with adjusted isoflurane. An activated LFP
pattern is not an awake attention task. The original mixed-sign response
finding remains bounded by that preparation.

## Placebo contrasts

- [Amanzio and Benedetti 1999](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.19-01-00484.1999)
  separates framing and prior-drug histories with naloxone tests. The endpoint is
  participant-controlled pain tolerance; receptor involvement does not identify
  opioid release or a complete neural route.
- [Benedetti et al. 2003](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.23-10-04315.2003)
  separates pain, motor and hormone cohorts. Negative motor instructions and
  conditioned hormone changes differ from unproved positive motor gain and
  finite suggestion-only hormone nulls. The conscious/unconscious partition
  remains an interpretation.
- [Wechsler et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1103319) keeps active,
  placebo, sham and no-intervention visits separate. Subjective improvement
  reports do not establish added placebo bronchodilation, precise equivalence
  or improvement from a measured baseline symptom score.
- [Albring et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0049477) separates
  conditioned and probability-instruction experiments, extracellular and
  intracellular IL-2 assays, reused observations and distinct schedules. A
  positive four-evocation protocol and a nonsignificant one-evocation protocol
  do not identify a minimum count. Missing unpaired drug/cue controls leave
  associative mediation distinct from the observed protocol effect.
