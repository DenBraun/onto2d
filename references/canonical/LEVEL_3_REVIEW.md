# Level 3 source review

Review all 50 [original biochemical source cards](../level-3.json). Levels and
phases organize the display; they do not establish a causal or historical
emergence sequence. The original catalogue remains a research input.

A disposition is complete only when every substantive assertion and parent/default
claim is supported, qualified or excluded. Actual publications, reading extent,
preparation, observable, uncertainty and shared-data dependencies belong to the
current claims. Source review does not independently reproduce the experiment.

All 50 cards have scoped dispositions; no Level-3 cards remain on the
[pending list](pending-review.json). Cards 3.10, 3.16, 3.30, 3.35, 3.37 and 3.47
retain explicit unresolved hypotheses, rather than unfinished source tasks.
Card 3.49 reuses the existing retinal evidence with its complete scope and
parent limits, without duplicating those studies. Completed disposition does
not mean that the original assertions are all supported or the experiments reproduced.

For each card:

1. Read the full original prose, actual parents and relevant canonical evidence.
   Do not inherit weights, necessity, minima or SOMA classifications as physics.
2. Select a finite system and observable. Read actual primary text, verify its
   identity/corrections, and retain exact reading limits and source conflicts.
3. Separate definitions, preparation, measurements and conditional inference.
   Reuse the same study identity and complete context across dependent cards.
4. Admit only justified relations. Distinguish supplied starting materials from
   measured formation; modern cellular systems do not establish prebiotic history.
5. Protect interpretation boundaries with meaningful checks. Remove a pending
   entry only after the whole original card has a disposition.
6. Rebuild and verify the current graph, registry and lazy browser projection
   within existing budgets. Preserve original inputs and frozen releases.
