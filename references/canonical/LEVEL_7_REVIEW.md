# Level 7 source review

All 25 social-system source cards have scoped decisions for 244 substantive assertions,
131 ordered proposed parents and all 75 original bibliography positions. The raw
catalogue remains an author-supplied draft; its evidence labels are not inherited
as scientific classifications. The complete decisions and source identities live in
[social-systems-review.json](social-systems-review.json).

Assertion decisions comprise 24 supported, 62 qualified, 90 excluded and 68 unresolved.
Parent decisions comprise 66 qualified, seven excluded and 58 unresolved. No original
parent is admitted as a universally necessary weighted social dependency. An unresolved
decision identifies an open question; an excluded universal claim is not a negative
experimental result. Completing these decisions does not reproduce the studies.

## Construction and evidence rules

The reconstruction starts with observable units, conditions and responses, not the
source catalogue’s proposed stage order. Each experiment or observation retains its
sample, task, intervention, controls, timing, endpoint and reading limit. Separate
analyses from the same people, animals or data are not independent replication.
Topic definitions organize these records through descriptive links. Only ten exact
preparation-bound intervention links are functional; no group label, correlation,
simulation or institutional document supplies a physical construction rule.

The contribution contains 124 entities, 124 claims, 192 relations, 36 study contexts
and 17 interpretation comparisons. The 36 contexts comprise 28 experimental/task
preparations, six observational preparations and two computational contexts from
24 publications. Experimental design labels do not turn within-task correlations
into randomized mechanisms. There are 94 new source records: 93 publications and
the original UN Charter as a primary document. Sixty-three source readings are
bibliographic metadata only; unread book chapters supply attribution, not empirical
support. Selected conceptual passages and correction notices retain their own scope.

Every original reference has an ordered reading disposition and a retrievable source.
Original editions, translations, reprints and catalogue-year discrepancies remain
distinct. Institutional rules are represented as definitions of the selected original
text. They do not establish implementation, compliance, legitimacy or effectiveness.

## Topic coverage

| Card | Topic | Current evidence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7.0 | Social Organization of Animals | Animal social organization is represented through finite, species-specific relational observations and interventions: cichlid aggression-defined hierarchy, vampire-bat sharing ties, bird foraging traditions, fish target choices and harvester-ant foraging regulation. These differ in membership, history, resources and observed times. Group structure and individual differences can both matter, while a common emergent substance, universal neural prerequisite, purely decentralized architecture and member-independent persistence are not established. |
| 7.1 | Social Roles and Stratification | Aggression-defined dominance, influence, learned competence, worker task and voluntarily conferred human prestige are distinguished rather than treated as interchangeable rank. Cichlid experiments support context-sensitive hierarchy formation and changing individual positions. They do not establish human authority, obligation, responsibility, durable positions surviving complete occupant replacement or one hierarchy required by all social coordination. |
| 7.2 | Recurrent Social Practices | Repeated social practice is a descriptive category for temporally recurring coordinated actions, with experimentally seeded bird foraging techniques and recurring bat-sharing ties as bounded animal examples. Technique transmission and nine-month retention do not establish every human practice, explicit expectations, automatic conflict reduction or universal dependence on hierarchy. Original practice-theory books remain attributed as unread conceptual sources. |
| 7.3 | Social Exchange and Resource Coordination | Physical resource transfer, task-specific food acquisition and normative human exchange are represented separately. Captive bat studies provide observed food-sharing relationships and competing explanations; ant studies provide supplied-cue and return-flow interventions, not measurements of a complete social metabolism. Redistribution, obligations, institutions, queueing and scalability remain conceptual or unresolved unless their own source-bound observations support them. |
| 7.4 | Collective Decision-Making | Collective choice is operationalized by a declared group endpoint rather than a single universal aggregation rule. Golden-shiner experiments vary untrained membership against fixed, conflicting target-training groups and distinguish a preference-dependent effect from a reversed-target null. Published spatial/network models and social-choice concepts remain separate from fish observations. First arrival, majority alignment, consensus, truth, legitimacy and future group coordination are different quantities. |
| 7.5 | Structures of Human Society | Human societal structures are a representation of specified relations, institutions and spatial aggregations, not one experimentally isolated ordering substance. Historical constitutive rules and observational urban indicators offer different evidence. Persistence across office holders, generations, territories and social strata needs its own longitudinal sampling; none is proved merely by calling society a pattern. |
| 7.6 | Collective Intelligence under Distributed Aggregation | Collective intelligence denotes task-bound group-performance structure or accuracy under specified aggregation, not a single group mind. The two Woolley laboratory cohorts supply fitted cross-task patterns; Lorenz's feedback experiment supplies a contrary case where convergence and confidence do not deliver matching collective improvement. Group composition, interaction, aggregation and inference metrics remain separate. |
| 7.7 | Superorganism Behavior in Insect Colonies | The colony category organizes experimentally perturbed harvester-ant foraging, local cue composition, observed departure timing and duration-dependent recovery. It does not combine all insect taxa, castes, task types and times into one measured colony organism. Foraging traffic, contacts and vestibule occupancy retain their own endpoints and nulls; chemical dose, necessary encounter threshold, complete feedback closure, construction, reproduction and colony-level irreducibility remain unestablished by these preparations. |
| 7.8 | Social Norms and Conventions | A convention is represented through the particular coordination and incentive setting in which it is observed; a moral or prescriptive norm is not identical to naming agreement. The naming experiment supplies finite adoption successes/failures after a committed minority is introduced, while punishment records show that sanctions need not improve cooperation. Shared expectations, reputation, exclusion and moral obligation need separate measurements. |
| 7.9 | Dynamics of Economic Markets | Market dynamics are reconstructed as transactions under explicitly supplied trading rules, values and information. Smith's classroom auctions provide conditional price/volume behavior; Hayek supplies a separate conceptual dispersed-knowledge argument. Neither is a complete model of production, financial crisis, trust, macroeconomic stability or a universal decentralized social regime. |
| 7.10 | Political and Governance Structures | Governance structures are represented as named constitutive offices, decision procedures and specified powers, separately from measured cooperation or realized authority. The original Charter supplies a documentary institutional example; common-pool experiments distinguish communication, monitoring, sanction costs, combined institutional trajectories and historically selected sanction choices. Definitions of jurisdiction or legitimacy do not themselves establish effective compliance or a universal route from informal norms to states. |
| 7.11 | Evolution of Complex Social Institutions | Institutional change is a source-bounded historical question about specified rules, offices, practices and their alteration. A constitutive document defines continuity and powers; short laboratory treatments reveal different outcomes under nominally similar institutions, sanction costs and prior-experience-dependent selection. These supply neither intergenerational reproduction, universal path dependence, an autonomous institutional force nor one constructor for law, religion, education, science and finance. |
| 7.12 | Cultural and Linguistic Evolutionary Change | Cultural change is represented through finite learner-to-learner language experiments and measure-specific migrant profiles, with historical cultural theory retained as bibliographic context. Transmission fidelity, structural alignment, task expressivity, cohort association and conditional transmission-model fit are different observables. These cases do not establish one historical evolutionary regime, a universal cultural replicator or an inevitable progression from institutions to language. |
| 7.13 | Morphogenesis of Cities and Urban Dynamics | Cities are operational spatial/population aggregations whose observed indicators and scaling depend on the national system, boundary, cutoff and proxy. Source-reported urban regularities are retained together with boundary-sensitive contrary evidence. Morphological formation, transport, segregation, infrastructure inertia and causal urban feedback need distinct data; no autonomous urban attractor, biological identity or universal growth law is inferred from aggregate correlations. |
| 7.14 | Digital Social Networks | Digital-network evidence separates randomized platform messages and score displays from observational rumor diffusion and conditional diffusion models. A site definition, one platform's experimental effect, a reconstructed retweet cascade and an inferred influence mechanism remain distinct. No universal recommendation algorithm, self-stabilizing digital regime or necessary cognitive hierarchy is established. |
| 7.15 | Formation of Influence Networks | Influence is represented through specific randomized exposure effects and a separately declared diffusion-model report. A following/friendship link, visible popularity, an estimated treatment effect and a persistent directed influence coefficient are not interchangeable. Network stabilization, generalized brokerage advantage and a universal influential elite remain unestablished. |
| 7.16 | Epistemic Markets and Knowledge Valuation | Knowledge valuation is a declared scope for attention, assessment and credibility processes, illustrated here by randomized public-rating effects and externally fact-checked diffusion. Approval, visibility, reputation, truth and evidential warrant remain distinct. Historical sociology/philosophy is bibliographic context; a common decentralized market mechanism across science, media, education and policy is not measured. |
| 7.17 | Cooperation and Conflict in Large-Scale Societies | Cooperation, sanctions, organized conflict and violence are distinct observables. Selected published public-goods/common-pool results constrain universal enforcement stories: communication can support bounded agreements, fines have costs and variable outcomes, institutional choices retain prior experience and antisocial punishment accompanies weaker contribution gains. Country background is observational. These games do not reconstruct wars, large alliances, political mobilization, intergenerational rivalry or a common society-wide regulatory dynamic. |
| 7.18 | Cultural Transmission, Recombination, and Idea Propagation | Cultural propagation is represented through changing laboratory language outputs and fact-checked platform cascades. A chosen content variant, its observable transmission, a reconstructed cascade and the hypothesis of a self-replicating meme retain separate roles. Variation and retention can be studied without admitting a universal memetic unit, fixed cultural fitness or source-independent replication mechanism. |
| 7.19 | Social Identity and Group Belonging | Social identity is represented through a bounded minimal-categorization allocation report, differentiated migrant affiliation/orientation profiles and an acute exclusion imaging task. Behavioral allocation, questionnaire affiliation, cultural-group classification and distress/BOLD are different measurements. These sources do not establish one persistent social-system identity property, necessary national symbolism or unavoidable out-group conflict. |
| 7.20 | Societal Feedback on Individual Neurodynamics | The proposed societal-to-neural link is bounded here by acute ordered social-exclusion imaging and already admitted rat maternal-environment observations. Social context, reported distress, BOLD, chromatin profiles and long-term neural plasticity retain different populations, times and evidence types. No direct universal downward/diagonal causal law, chronic institutional control variable or minimum neural carrier is established. |
| 7.21 | Sociogenesis of Shared Information Environments | Shared information environments are represented as source-specific distributions of media salience, visible platform cues and fact-checked rumor exposure. The Chapel Hill media/public correlation, randomized platform response and observational diffusion are different preparations. A societal attractor, common credibility field, centralized-control independence or fixed sociogenetic construction order remains a hypothesis rather than a measured system state. |
| 7.22 | Transculturation and Hybridization of Traditions | Transculturation is a declared historical-cultural research scope, illustrated by heterogeneous migrant-group profiles and bounded laboratory reorganization of transmitted labels. A mixed or intermediate measurement does not alone prove reciprocal historical transformation, creation of a novel tradition or irreducibility to cultural sources. Colonial power, unequal contact and adaptation claims require separately identified historical evidence. |
| 7.23 | Mass Cognitive Distortion Regimes | The proposed mass-distortion regime remains a hypothesis bounded by fact-check-defined diffusion, randomized rating bias, a numerical-estimation feedback experiment and partisan attitude responses. A factual error, preference, ideological extremity, approval score, retweet and confidence judgment are distinct. No persistent societal attractor, common cognitive pathology or universal network-amplification mechanism is established. |
| 7.24 | Global Governance and Supranational Institutions | Transnational governance is reconstructed through specific historical constitutive organs, procedures and powers, rather than an undifferentiated global object. The 1945 Charter distinguishes organizational design, sovereign-member principles and asymmetric decision/enforcement provisions. These definitions differ from current amended rules, observed compliance, legitimacy, democratic authority and causal effectiveness; the original interdependence/regime works retain their actual reading boundaries. |

## Contrasts and reporting limits

- Fish hierarchy, food sharing, bird traditions and insect coordination retain species,
  sampled interactions, partial turnover and selection limits. First fish arrival is a
  choice readout, not equal voting or decision accuracy. The Couzin supplement’s trial
  totals, Aplin’s option labels and Greene’s hydrocarbon units contain explicit conflicts.
  They supply no universal interaction threshold, stable role constructor or proof of irreducibility.
- Group task factors, numerical-estimation feedback and naming-game adoption are different
  outcomes. Lorenz’s main-text footnote reports slight, partly significant collective-error
  improvement despite the headline account; confidence and convergence are not equivalent
  to accuracy. The naming experiment’s approximately 25% transition is preparation-specific.
- Classroom auctions operate inside supplied trading rules. Communication, sanction costs,
  monitoring information, imposed combinations and endogenous institution selection remain
  separate in the final Ostrom paper. Prior experience and self-selection constrain the
  institutional contrasts. Herrmann’s high-contributor punishment and nonsignificant pool
  results do not prove universal harm or exact absence of benefit; country associations are
  observational. Urban fits depend on indicators, city definitions and the reviewed source version.
- Language transmission separates fidelity from expressive distinctions. Randomized voting
  messages, rating signals and offers of opposing-view exposure have different endpoints;
  they do not identify one influence parameter. Group allocation and social-exclusion BOLD
  differ from belief, empathy, physical pain and chronic societal control of neurodynamics.
- Fact-checked rumor diffusion has a declared classification criterion, but sharing is not
  a direct belief measurement. A popularity score does not measure epistemic truth. Migrant
  group contrasts and fitted transmission models do not isolate individual learning paths
  or define an ethnic essence. Hybridization requires specified variants, contact directions
  and histories; a cross-sectional trait profile does not reconstruct every cultural exchange.
- The Kirby 2017 notice corrects Supporting Table S7; the replacement and full SI remain
  unread. Mesoudi’s signed 2018 notice corrects swapped Table 4 closeness summaries. Their
  notices are reachable from the affected claims. Corrected supporting-method material in
  Woolley is distinguished from unreplayed raw scores and psychometric fits.

## Remaining scientific work

Independent scientific review, source-specific unread controls and raw-data reproduction
remain open. Chronic social exposure, intergenerational institutional persistence, cultural
hybridization, individual belief change and unique neural mediation need their own eligible
measurements and competing explanations. A future cross-domain construction test must
define its units, interventions, observation horizon, comparator and failure criterion
before inspecting a new outcome. The current graph admits no universal social carrier
minimum, parent weight, ontology-wide attractor or society-to-brain law.
