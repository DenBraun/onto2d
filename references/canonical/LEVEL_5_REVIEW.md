# Level 5 source review

All 34 [organismal catalogue cards](../level-5.json) have explicit scientific scope contracts. Each substantive assertion and every ordered parent occurrence is supported, qualified, excluded or unresolved. This accounts for the original proposals; it does not establish every proposal, reproduce an experiment or make display levels a universal developmental or evolutionary sequence.

The organismal contribution contains **384 entities, 378 claims, 499 relations, 129 study contexts and 26 comparisons**. The relations comprise 428 descriptive and 71 functional-support links. Its 53 publication records comprise 51 main publications and two correction notices; already admitted evidence is reused within its existing reading scope. The study contexts comprise 107 primary experiments, 12 primary observations and 10 computational analyses. Multiple preparations or cards from one paper do not create independent replication.

The 236 assertion dispositions comprise 41 supported, 85 qualified, 68 excluded and 42 unresolved. The 167 parent dispositions comprise 100 qualified, 9 excluded and 58 unresolved. These are coverage counts, not confidence scores or causal weights. No parent disposition by itself creates a physical dependency.

The [scope contracts](../../models/causal-emergence/canonical/level-five.mjs) retain individual decisions and reasons. The [organismal ledger](organismal-review.json) retains preparation, intervention, observable, interpretation, uncertainty and actual reading extent; [canonical claims](graph.json) retain passage locators. The matrix selects primary sources rather than duplicating every claim.

## Scientific boundaries

Definitions, observed structures, intervention responses and computational explanations remain separate. Functional links require a supported input-to-readout contrast within the actual preparation. A supplied cell, organoid, animal or patient cohort is not a reconstructed origin. Markers, gene expression, morphology, tracer transport, metabolic labeling, physiological function and clinical outcomes are distinct endpoints.

Original phase/type labels, necessary-parent codes, weights, carrier identifiers, minima and saturation defaults are excluded from active scientific semantics. Unread supplements, unresolved reporting conflicts and unknown mechanisms stay explicit. Source checking and software consistency checks are not independent laboratory reproduction, complete raw-data replay or an exhaustive correction audit.

## Remaining scientific obligations

- Obtain independent scientific review of primary-source interpretation, including unread controls and recorded within-paper conflicts. Passing a closed data contract does not detect every shared reading error.
- Test candidate organization patterns against whole new publications with predictions and failure criteria specified before outcome reading. Shared papers, cohorts, preparations and analyses remain dependent evidence.
- Measure complete matched paths before assigning neural-to-hormonal-to-organ closure, normal-tissue resource allocation, whole-organ function or a unique matrix/niche mechanism.
- Separate transient markers, phenotype persistence, lineage retention, tissue recovery and long-term function. Preserved endpoints and negative interventions constrain universal necessity claims.
- Operationalize immune set points, thresholds and resource budgets before asserting them. A hysteresis test needs both driver directions, adequate relaxation controls and repeated comparison at the same driver; persistence or partial rescue alone is insufficient.
- Require direct exchange, metabolic flux or downstream decoding measurements before replacing proxy observations with end-to-end transport, energetic or information claims. No universal construction rule is admitted by this review.
