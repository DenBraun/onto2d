# Canonical interaction comparison

Under which declared conditions do interactions select an organizational regime, maintain a specified property, or change a measured operation, and where do these proposed correspondences fail?

This is a purposive, retrospective comparison of existing literature records. The system view contains descriptions of studied systems, not admitted physical instances. Support judgments below are explicit interpretations of the cited records, not automated scientific verification.

Scientific cases, properties and proposed mappings are authoritative in [the canonical graph](../../references/canonical/graph.json). ## Current finding

The selected cases support several bounded distinctions rather than one universal organization law. Conditions discriminate organizational regimes in particular models/preparations; retention, kinetic resistance and supplied recurrent operation have different evidential requirements; state or arrangement changes can alter measured response without demonstrating a universal efficiency gain.

Contrary and unresolved cases are central results: CETCH 1.0 does not compose from working steps into a complete recurrent turn; batch RNA amplification depletes supplied substrates; negative CAPN cooperativity limits a universal amplification interpretation; isolated colloid data do not establish long-range attraction; Ddx4 partition does not measure catalytic benefit; and cage controls do not uniquely identify substrate channeling. These distinctions motivate conditional compatibility and maintenance hypotheses, not a new physical ancestry chain.

Retrospective challenge cases expose limits of transfer: AlScN supplies a state-associated response sign change in a specific film preparation; CETCH contrasts designed topology with supplied finite operation and a reported earlier stall; vesicles separate exchange, retention and primer-extension assays; and the colloid-pair null prevents treating every interaction measurement as assembly evidence. No common numerical threshold, causal/emergence score, prospective generalization result or independently reviewed scientific conclusion follows.

Separate new-source readings of Banerjee2017, Boekhoven2015 and Drobot2018 test the operational distinctions outside the selected atlas publications. These checks retain the case-specific readout, comparator and limitations; their result-bearing titles/search exposure and retrospective target selection prevent a blind prospective validation claim. Full review records remain outside canonical physical-claim admission.

## Evaluation boundary

All selected publications and reported outcomes were available during source review and informed this purposive selection and protocol. No case in this pilot is a genuinely unseen prospective test. Case annotations are literature interpretations, not independent acquisition or numerical replay.

Keep each primary publication and its preparation/readout/model records in one dependency group. Use AlScN switching, the isolated colloid-pair null, all CETCH versions and all selected Mansy vesicle preparations as retrospective challenge groups; the other ten publication groups develop the comparisons. Group disjointness is a leakage check, not proof that different publications have no shared assumptions or that the selection is representative.

Independent scientific review: **not performed**. Prospective evaluation on unseen studies: **not performed**. Scientific accuracy, emergence and causal scores: **not assigned**. The challenge partition checks transfer across separate publication groups retrospectively; it cannot recreate an unseen-study test after the findings have been read.

## Candidate families and decision criteria

These criteria define bounded comparisons. A counterexample to an overextension is not automatically a refutation of the narrower candidate. Missing or unread measurements remain inconclusive.

### Conditions and organizational regimes

Eligibility: A declared intervention or computational scan changes a specified condition and reports a discriminating structural/order observable. Keep model ensembles separate from physical preparations; distinguish jointly changed conditions from an isolated causal intervention.

Scoped prediction: Within the specified comparison, the reported condition change selects distinguishable states under the stated organizational observable. This is a case-specific retrospective prediction; it assigns no universal threshold and predicts no unmeasured dynamics.

Support:

- `regime-observed`: The source reports distinct organizational readouts under the declared conditions, with preparation and observation/model boundaries retained.

Contrary observation or overextension test:

- `regime-unchanged`: Within an eligible matched test, the specified discriminator remains unchanged across the claimed switching range at the declared resolution. Such a result challenges that bounded prediction, not all possible condition dependence.

Inconclusive:

- `regime-unisolated`: Readouts are absent, conditions/constructs change together, or a model-assisted classification does not isolate the proposed physical switching mechanism.

Overextension to avoid: A component list uniquely fixes organization, every environmental change induces a transition, or a phase label alone supplies physical kinetics.

### Persistence through barriers, exchange constraints and supplied resources

Eligibility: Specify what persists or recovers, its preparation, a perturbation/comparator if available, and a finite observation window. Separate measured durations, fitted/extrapolated rates and computational clocks. Barrier, exchange and replenishment hypotheses are different mechanisms.

Scoped prediction: A specified target remains detectable or recurrently operational over the reported finite horizon under the stated boundary/resource conditions; loss or altered persistence in a relevant comparison constrains its maintenance requirements. Mechanism identification requires additional controls.

Support:

- `persistence-bounded`: The source establishes finite retention, limited conversion, recovery or repeated operation under declared conditions, without converting non-detection or extrapolation into indefinite stability.
- `persistence-inferred-kinetics`: Data-dependent rate fits/extrapolations support a preparation-conditional kinetic persistence account with the measured input, model and uncertainty retained. This is evidence about inferred kinetics; no direct observation of the extrapolated lifetime is claimed.

Contrary observation or overextension test:

- `persistence-loss`: An actually reported loss, stall or depletion contradicts the overextension from topology, structural order or individually working steps to unconditional persistent operation. Do not label missing measurements as observed loss.

Inconclusive:

- `persistence-mechanism-unresolved`: Persistence horizon is unmeasured, the relevant control is missing, a lifetime is extrapolated, or the evidence does not isolate a claimed barrier, exchange or replenishment mechanism.

Overextension to avoid: A cycle is autonomous, every ordered structure persists indefinitely, a fitted extrapolation is an observed lifetime, or all persistence mechanisms share one numerical stability measure.

### Organization-associated changes in operation

Eligibility: Require a declared functional/response observable and a comparator. State the anticipated direction or qualitative discriminator for each case; preserve active fractions, preparation differences and possible common-environment explanations.

Scoped prediction: In the selected matched comparison, the declared state/arrangement change is associated with its specified response difference (including sign reversal or suppression). Spatial proximity alone cannot identify a mechanism or predict improvement.

Support:

- `operation-compared`: An actual comparison reports the named response difference under stated controls. This supports only that scoped response relation, not a unique pathway or universal benefit.

Contrary observation or overextension test:

- `operation-no-universal-gain`: A measured suppressive, null or sign-changing response challenges a universal monotonic-gain extension. Do not treat a missing functional assay as a measured null.
- `operation-composition-failure`: The source reports successful individual transformations but incomplete operation when combined, challenging guaranteed composition of working steps. Keep the preparation and actual extent of failure/control review explicit.

Inconclusive:

- `operation-unmeasured`: Only structure, partition, mobility or pair interaction is measured; the proposed functional benefit or assembly operation is not measured.
- `operation-mechanism-unresolved`: A response difference exists but alternative controls, unmatched active populations or multiple changed conditions prevent attribution to the proposed isolated mechanism.
- `operation-comparator-missing`: A functional outcome is measured, but no matched organizational comparator identifies an organization-specific change or benefit. Observed operation alone is insufficient for this candidate.

Overextension to avoid: More organization necessarily improves operation, co-localization proves substrate channeling, or partition alone demonstrates catalytic benefit.

Evidence distinctions (exact field-to-claim mappings remain in the JSON):

- **reported-observation:** The source observes time-dependent H-C-OH infrared-band loss in the declared dark host-matrix preparations. Its first-order fitted half-lives are retained separately as a measurement inference.
- **reported-observation:** Assigned H-C-OD bands are reported unchanged over an unspecified extended observation period.
- **model-result:** The source reduced reaction-path/WKB calculation predicts a near-0-K H half-life of 122 min and a D half-life over 1200 yr.
- **not-established:** No numerical experimental D half-life or kinetic isotope ratio is established; the model does not supply a matrix-specific account of all four hosts.
- **measurement-inference:** First-order analysis of the measured H-C-OH band loss gives source half-lives: at 11 K Ar 1.8 h, Kr 2.0 h, Xe 2.3 h, N2 7.0 h; at 15 K Ar 1.9 h; at 20 K Ar 1.7 h and Kr 2.0 h. These are source fits, not a new fit or directly measured D lifetime.

Comparison judgments:

- `persistence` / `persistence-inferred-kinetics`: **supports-scoped-instance**. Measured H band loss and its source first-order fits support preparation-specific finite kinetic estimates. Deuterated non-decay is excluded from this positive assessment because its observation horizon is unspecified.
- `persistence` / `persistence-mechanism-unresolved`: **inconclusive**. The D observation horizon is unspecified, so no numerical lower lifetime bound follows. The separate reduced WKB interpretation does not establish full-dimensional, matrix-specific dynamics reproducing all host effects.

Limits:

- Selected published main-text/Methods passages and selected actual publisher-supplement passages were read; most coordinate/energy tables and raw spectra were not reanalysed.
- The experiment and associated calculation are different evidence roles within one publication. The 1200 yr D value is theoretical, not an observed retention window.
- Missing temperature/host cells, errors, replicates, detection limits and numeric D observation window remain unknown.
- Visible-light photochemistry is separate from dark kinetics; IR loss does not directly count tunnelling events or certify every quantum state.
- This is a persistence/chemical-conversion case. It does not independently demonstrate switching between collective organizational regimes.
- This mixed-evidence case retains experiment and computation as distinct observation kinds and study contexts within one shared publication group.
