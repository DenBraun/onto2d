# Code of Conduct

Onto2D welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience and viewpoints.
This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where
people participate in or represent the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience, including newcomers.
- Critique claims, evidence and code rather than the person presenting them.
- Explain disagreements clearly, accept corrections and respect boundaries.
- Give appropriate credit and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Scientific
disagreement and reporting negative results are welcome when expressed
respectfully.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to **denbritov@gmail.com**, monitored by
Denis Britov ([@DenBraun](https://github.com/DenBraun)). Include links or context
when safe to share, and say how you would prefer to be contacted. Do not post
private allegations or personal information in public issues.

The maintainer will review reports, protect reporter information as far as
practical, and respond proportionately. Actions may include requesting a change
in behavior, removing inappropriate content, a warning, or temporarily or
permanently restricting project participation. Reports and requests to reconsider
a decision use the same private contact. There is currently one maintainer;
the project cannot offer an independent appeals panel or a response-time SLA.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).
